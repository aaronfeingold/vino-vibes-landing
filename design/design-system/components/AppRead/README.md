# AppRead

What it read: the count of wines and dishes, dish chips to say what you're having, and the list with bottle and glass prices. A misread entry is tinted vv-warning and says what to fix. One gradient button: "Pick for me".

Sample restaurant: Maison Lune (invented). Wines are real producers with representative prices. The screen is 390 × 844 and appears on the site inside a PhoneFrame.

Code: `src/components/landing/screens/AppRead.tsx`, built from `Screen`, `TopBar`, `SectionLabel` and `Row` in `parts.tsx`.
