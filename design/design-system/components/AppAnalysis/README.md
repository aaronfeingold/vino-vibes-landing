# AppAnalysis

List analysis, in the system's voice rather than "I read". Four steps, each with its result: extracted from the photos, matched to the wine database (problems surface here), compared with your palate, saved to your library. Then dish chips for what you're having, a "Needs a look" row, and one gradient button: "Pick for me". Replaces AppRead.

Sample restaurant: Maison Lune (invented). Wines are real producers with representative prices. The screen is 390 × 844 and appears on the site inside a PhoneFrame. Design: `design/canvas/Vino Vibes App v2.dc.html`, option 2g.

Code: `src/components/landing/screens/AppAnalysis.tsx`, built from the parts in `parts.tsx`.
