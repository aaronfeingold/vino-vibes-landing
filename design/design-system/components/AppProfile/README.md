# AppProfile

Your profile, the centre of the app. Opens on a one-line portrait. Palate, Lifestyles and Experiences are tabs; Palate is a style map of every wine they've had (light to bold, crisp to rich) with their sweet spot shaded. Then what they're drifting toward and recent wines. Sits above the Snap · Library · You tab bar. Replaces AppMemory.

Sample restaurant: Maison Lune (invented). Wines are real producers with representative prices. The screen is 390 × 844 and appears on the site inside a PhoneFrame. Design: `design/canvas/Vino Vibes App v2.dc.html`, option 2b.

Code: `src/components/landing/screens/AppProfile.tsx`, built from the parts in `parts.tsx`.
