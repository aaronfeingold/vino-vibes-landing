# Card

The site's one container: `vv-surface`, `radius-card` (20 px), 28 px padding, no border and no shadow.

- **Offer card** (Partner with us, "What you get"): title at 18 px 700, body in `vv-text2` at 16 px, 1.625 leading. Three in a row from 768 px.
- **Stat card** (Home, "Built at New Orleans tables"): a 48 px 800 number over a one-line label in `vv-text2`, centred, max 400 px wide. Only real numbers go here; the quote card stays hidden until there's a real diner quote.
- **Restaurant strip** (end of Home): title, one line, and the outline "Partner with us" button with an arrow, linking to `/partners`. 28 by 32 px padding, max 900 px.

On a `vv-band` section the card still reads, because `vv-surface` is lighter than `vv-band`.
