60-SECOND LESSON
Barolo
PLACE
Langhe hills, Piedmont. Autumn fog, limestone and clay underfoot.
GRAPE
Nebbiolo, named for that fog. Pale in the glass, fierce in the mouth.
TIME
At least 38 months of ageing before release, 18 of them in wood.
TASTE
Tar and roses, sour cherry, grip that softens over years.
